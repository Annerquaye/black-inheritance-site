Who we are

Too much of what Black founders build is sold away at the moment it becomes valuable. We exist to give those businesses a permanent home.

We are a family of companies under one patient owner. We take long-term stakes, keep management in place, and back them with capital, governance and a shared network. What we build, we intend to hand down.

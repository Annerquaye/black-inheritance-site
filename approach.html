How a partnership begins

From first meeting to forever

Get to know each other

We spend months, sometimes years, with founders before we discuss terms.

Agree on a fair price

We pay a fair price and structure deals so founders can keep a stake.

Keep what works

Management stays. The name, the culture and the team stay.

Build for the long term

We add capital, governance and group support, then measure progress in decades.

What we look for

Acquisition criteria

Profitable

An established business with a record of profit, typically three years or more.

Black-founded or led

Founded, owned or led by Black entrepreneurs, or serving Black communities.

Built to last

Products people need, loyal customers and a reason to exist in 30 years.

Leaders who stay

A team that wants to keep running the business with a long-term partner.

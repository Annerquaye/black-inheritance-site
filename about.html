Patient capital · Generational ownership

An inheritance,
built on purpose.

Generational wealth does not happen by accident. We started Black Inheritance to build it deliberately, and to share it.

Our story

Our founder grew up watching good Black-owned businesses close when their founders retired, or sell to buyers who stripped out what made them special.

Black Inheritance began with family capital and one simple rule: buy well, run it better, never sell. A decade later the group spans six sectors. The rule has not changed.

We are a holding company, not a fund. There is no exit date, and the owners of our capital share our horizon.

Mission

To keep great Black-owned businesses in Black hands, and help them grow for the next generation.

Vision

A lasting base of ownership that families, employees and communities can inherit.

Values

How we steward

Governance

How decisions are made

Board of Directors

Seven directors, four of them independent, oversee strategy and capital allocation.

Founders' Council

Founders of portfolio companies advise the board and keep a vote on culture.

Family Charter

A written charter commits the group to long-term ownership and sets out how it passes on.

Timeline
  1. 2015

    Founded with family capital and a first acquisition: a 40-year-old property business.

  2. 2018

    Launched Kente Capital, our first business built from scratch.

  3. 2020

    Opened the group to outside long-term partners, including two family offices and an endowment.

  4. 2022

    Introduced an employee ownership plan across every company in the group.

  5. 2025

    Reached eight operating companies in six sectors, with none ever sold.
